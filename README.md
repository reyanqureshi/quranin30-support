  ## FAQ

  **How do I reset my reading plan?**
  Go to Settings → Reset Progress. You can then create a new plan with a different duration.

  **Can I change my reading pace?**
  Yes! Go to Settings → Adjust Plan to change your target number of days without losing your current
  progress.

  **How do I restore my data on a new device?**
  Sign in with the same account on your new device, then go to Settings → Cloud Sync → Restore.

  **How do I enable prayer time notifications?**
  Go to Settings → Prayer Times and enable "Prayer Time Alerts." Make sure notifications are allowed
  in your iPhone's Settings → QuranIn30.

  **The athan sound isn't playing. What should I do?**
  Make sure "Athan Sound" is toggled on under Prayer Times in Settings. The app will download the
  audio file on first enable — ensure you have an internet connection.

  **How do I cancel my subscription?**
  Open the Settings app on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → QuranIn30 → Cancel.

  ## Privacy

  QuranIn30 does not sell or share your personal data. Your reading progress and bookmarks are stored
  locally on your device and optionally synced to your account via Firebase.
